Our Beliefs

We are an Arabic church in Cincinnati, Ohio, USA. Our aim is to grow as a community rooted in the teachings of our Lord Jesus Christ and the Holy Bible, practically embodying what it means to be Ecclesia.

We are committed to embracing the infallibility and credibility of the Holy Bible as we strengthen our faith together.

We believe in the incarnation of the Son—the Word of God became flesh—and how it continues to shape our lives.

As we grow, we eagerly anticipate the work, gifts, and leadership of the Holy Spirit to the collective body of Christ from different backgrounds and denominations.

We believe that we have a call to change our community and to have a meaningful impact in the lives of those around us.

We affirm the sanctity of marriage between a man and a woman.

We affirm the sanctity of life.

As we move forward, we embrace the belief that Jesus left the church as a continuation of His incarnation on earth, sending us to be Flames everywhere.

Our watchword: "Let your light shine before others..." (Matthew 5:16).
